Project Wiggle Keyboard

The keyboard appears to offer effortless control over technology, but it subtly shapes our behavior through hidden digital structures. In my redesign, the keycaps unpredictably spring out of place, breaking the intuitive relationship between user and object. This unpredictable behavior challenges the assumption that technology is a neutral, controllable tool and makes the user aware of their dependence on it. By turning a familiar device into an autonomous, disruptive entity, the design provokes reflection on control, autonomy, and the hidden influence of digital systems in daily life.
